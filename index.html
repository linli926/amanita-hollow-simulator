▸ METHODS — MODEL & ALGORITHMS

SEIRD compartmental model

β = R₀γ, σ = 1/latent, γ = 1/infectious, p = CFR. Integrated with classical 4th-order Runge–Kutta (dt = 0.1 d).

Final size & herd immunity

Solved by Newton's method. Holds for any latent-period distribution.

Growth rate (Euler–Lotka)

Links the early exponential growth rate r to R₀ for exponentially distributed latent (L) and infectious (D) periods.

Gillespie stochastic simulation

Exact simulation of the continuous-time Markov chain. For N > 8,000 a binomial tau-leap (dt = 0.25 d) is used instead.

Probability of a major outbreak

Branching-process approximation: each early case's offspring are geometric with mean R. Compare it with the ensemble's bimodal final-size histogram.

▸ METHODS — INFERENCE

Observation model

ιt = daily new infections from the SEIR ODE. Variance μ + μ²/k allows over-dispersion. The truth is one stochastic (Gillespie / tau-leap) realisation.

Maximum likelihood

θ = (log R₀, logit ρ, log I₀, log k), optimised with the Nelder–Mead simplex from several starts.

Profile likelihood CI

Nuisance parameters are re-optimised at each R₀. The forecast envelope spans the trajectories of all R₀ inside the interval.

Misspecification adjustment

The truth is a stochastic epidemic, so its residuals around the ODE mean are over-dispersed (φ̂) and serially correlated (r̂₁). Scaling the cut-off by ĉ (quasi-likelihood) raised empirical coverage of the nominal 95% interval from ≈70% to ≈90% in a 120-outbreak simulation study.

Growth-rate regression

Poisson GLM fitted by iteratively reweighted least squares; quasi-Poisson SE. Converted with R₀ = (1+rL)(1+rD).

Instantaneous reproduction number

Λs = Σ ys−uwu with the SEIR generation interval w (hypo-exponential). Window 7 days.

The drug

Volvarin (a fictional name) is an antifungal already licensed for other fungal infections. Lab tests at Sporeford suggest it may also act on the Amanita spores, so it is being repurposed. That is usually the first thing tried in a new outbreak, because an existing drug can be tested in weeks while a vaccine takes months.

The trial

Patients already hospitalised with severe Amanita infection are randomised 1:1 to Volvarin + standard care or placebo + standard care. The outcome is time from randomisation to death. Patients still alive at the end of follow-up, or lost to follow-up, are censored. This tests a treatment for the sick, which is different from the vaccine in the Vaccine Lab, which prevents infection in the healthy.

▸ METHODS — SURVIVAL ANALYSIS

Data-generating model

Weibull proportional hazards; staggered uniform accrual; administrative + exponential dropout censoring. The baseline hazard is calibrated by bisection so that the average placebo-arm 60-day mortality over the patient mix (age, comorbidity) equals the setting. By default that setting is derived from your pathogen: 60-day mortality ≈ 0.9 × CFR ÷ 10% hospitalised.

Kaplan–Meier & Greenwood

CIs on the log(−log) scale. Median CI from where the band crosses 0.5 (Brookmeyer–Crowley).

Log-rank test

Cox proportional hazards

Breslow ties; Newton–Raphson with step-halving; Wald CIs; likelihood-ratio test; Harrell's C-index.

Sample size

Number of deaths needed; checked against Monte Carlo power from repeated simulated trials.

▸ METHODS — METAPOPULATION MODEL

Coupled SEIRD villages

Each village is a stochastic SEIRD (binomial tau-leap, dt = 0.25 d). bk = your interventions × events. C = row-stochastic mixing.

Gravity-model mobility

Travel bans cut a village's outside mixing by 85%.

Next-generation matrix

The true network Rₜ is the spectral radius of K (power iteration), shown in the after-action report against your Cori estimate.

Counterfactual

At the end, 30 replicate epidemics with identical hidden parameters, seeding and events but no interventions give the deaths you were up against. Your grade is your deaths relative to that median.
